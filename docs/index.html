Family app

One app to replace the pile of apps: meal planning that knows who is at the table, a larder that fills itself in, jobs that fit the evenings you actually have, and a shopping list you can trust.

Try the demo live

The whole thing, running in your browser. Set your family up, tick who is in for dinner, confirm what is in the cupboard, and watch the shopping list change underneath you. Nothing leaves this browser unless you choose to sign in — until then it is all local, and Start over clears it.

The build plan

Stack decisions and why, the architecture, the larder and tasks designs, four phases, and an honest register of what could still go wrong.

Google Calendar runbook

Eight steps to wire Calendar access through Supabase auth, including the two that catch everybody out.

Check your Google setup

Step 8 of the runbook, as a page you can just open. Signs in for real and reports the three things that matter — session, refresh token, and whether it can actually read a calendar.

The demo has no model behind it. Generating a meal plan or reading a brain-dump into jobs needs an API key, and a key shipped to a web page is a key given away. So those two buttons are disabled here. Everything else — profiles, the week's table, the larder, the jobs scheduler, the pack solver — is the real code, running the same modules the server does. Clone the repo and set a key to see the AI half.
